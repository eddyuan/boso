Why a shared bar and not a leaderboard
×
A ranking publishes who is active near you.
Within a few streets that is a directory of your neighbours, which undoes the whole point of blurring where posts appear.
×
Rank is relative, so somebody has to fall.
Everything else here is absolute: bond XP never decays, missions don’t streak, mood always recovers.
×
Seeded accounts would make it a lie you can read.
A board whose rungs are bots is a false claim about activity, stated as a number.
✓
One bar has none of those problems.
No losing position, no directory, and nothing to fake — only real accounts count toward it.
