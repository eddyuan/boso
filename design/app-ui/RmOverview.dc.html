Roadmap screens
Every user-facing item on /admin/roadmap, drawn in the app’s own kit. Ordered by the loop each phase closes: fill the world, close the loop, earn the return, then give long-term players something to chase. Redrawn 2026-09-19 against what actually shipped — several of these changed shape during the build, and two features were replaced outright.
Gate expression, never reach
Levels may lock cosmetics, treasure tiers, errand range and titles. They must never lock who you can see, meet or talk to. Charging people days of grinding for access to other people attacks the exact thing that would have made them stay.
Pay for the behaviour you want to be true
The two largest awards are making a new friend (30) and answering what your pet asked (25). Daily care is 4 — a floor, not the engine. If tapping a food bowl were the fastest way to level, that would become the game.
Bots fill the room, never the friendship
Seeded accounts may make a neighbourhood look inhabited. They are never the other half of a reciprocal bond — no playdates, and they never count toward a neighbourhood goal.
Levels never decay. Mood does.
Progress you earned is permanent, so leaving for a fortnight is never punished. Mood is the thing that droops and recovers; it creates the tug to return without taking anything away.
What changed since the first pass
×
The weekly leaderboard is gone.
A per-area ranking publishes who is most active within a few streets, makes progress relative so one person rises only as another falls, and reads as false wherever seeded accounts fill its rungs. Replaced by a single shared bar — RmNeighbourhoodGoal.
×
Missions lost their weekly prize.
No cumulative “15 missions” tracker and no cosmetic at the end. Three a day, fixed at midnight, and the reward shown is whatever the bond already pays for that same act — one table, so the two can’t drift apart.
✓
The empty map became a feature.
New screens for the case nobody designed for: no posts nearby, so show the neighbourhood itself. Venues are real, so nothing is faked — RmMapPlaces, RmPlaceThread, RmPlaceThreadEmpty.

Two things these drawings can’t show
×
No screen here has been seen on a device.
Every surface is built and type-checked against a verified API shape, but layout, spacing and dark mode are unrendered. Treat the drawings as intent, not as a screenshot.
×
No scheduled job has run on a scheduler.
The diary, the morning digest, the comeback nudge and the classifier have only ever been called directly. Anything in these screens that arrives “overnight” is unproven end to end.
