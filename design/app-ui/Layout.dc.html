Rules
✓Side padding is 16 on every screen — set it once on the screen container, never again on children.
✓Sections are separated by 24. Anything closer belongs to the same section.
✓Scrolling tabs end with 104 of bottom padding so the floating bar never covers the last row.
✓A card owns its padding; rows inside it own only their own vertical rhythm.
✕Don't pad a screen and then pad its first card again — that reads as 32 and looks broken.
✕Don't invent 10, 14, 18 or 22 for new work. Round to the nearest step on the scale.
✕Don't let the tab bar stretch full width or sit flush to the bottom edge.
✕Don't put a shadow on cards inside a list — shadows are for floating things only (tab bar, map cards).
